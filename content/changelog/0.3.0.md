title: Everything in one place
---

- Uploads keep going while you look around. Drop a pile of clips, hit Upload, and go watch something. Leaving the page doesn't cancel them anymore.
- Uploading is one button now: drop your files, fix any titles, press Upload. Each clip spins while it waits, fills a bar while it goes up, and spins again while it's being prepared.
- The bar along the bottom shows what's going on anywhere on the site: who's in the theater and how your uploads are doing. Click either one for the controls.
- Switching tabs slides between pages, and a long jump flies past the tabs in between.
