- Upload clips straight from your browser. Big files resume if your connection drops.
- The library updates itself — new clips appear as they finish processing, no reloading.
- The theater. Whoever gets there first picks what plays and everyone else's video follows along, in sync. Hand control to anyone with one click.
- Chat and react in the theater. Reactions float up over the video.
- Comment on any clip, and tag clips with the game and who was in it.
- Click any tag, game or name to filter the library. The filtered view is a link you can send.
