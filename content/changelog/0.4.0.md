title: Make it yours
---

- You have a profile now. Pick a name, a colour and a picture, write a little bio, and change them whenever you like. Your picture and colour show up next to everything you post, and clicking anyone's name takes you to their page: their clips, the clips they're in, and what they've said.
- The clips page is all new. Flip between New, Trending, Top and Random, search for anything (even something someone said in a comment), and filter by game, tag or who's in it. It keeps loading as you scroll, and the theater has the same thing under the player.
- Like clips, and see who liked yours.
- The bell in the bottom bar tells you when someone likes or comments on your clips, tags you in one, or @mentions you. Type @ in a comment or in theater chat to call someone out.
- Been away a while? The top of the clips page catches you up on what you missed.
- Collections: make lists like "Kobe fails", let everyone add to them if you want, and play a whole collection in the theater.
- Share a clip from an exact moment, and comments remember where in the clip they were posted.
- Put the site on your phone's home screen from the menu under your picture. Sign out lives there too, and the "online" button up top shows who's around and who's in the theater.
- The theater and bell buttons along the bottom bar are proper icons now.
