title: Big uploads stick the landing
---

- Uploading a big pile of clips at once could leave a bunch of them marked "Could not process this video" even though nothing was wrong with them. The site was checking each file a split second too early, before storage had let go of it, and giving up after three quick tries. Now it waits a few seconds between tries, and your upload list says "Hit a snag, retrying…" while it does. If any of your clips failed in the last import, upload them again and they'll go through.
