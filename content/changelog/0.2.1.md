title: Line 'em up
---

- The theater has a queue. Anyone watching can add clips from the grid under the player, and whoever's in control plays them when the room's ready. Nothing starts on its own.
- Whoever's in control can drag the queue around, bump things up or down, or play any of it right away.
- Opening the theater puts you straight in the room. No Join button.
- New layout: queue on the left, the video in the middle, chat on the right. Hide either side to make the video bigger.
- Everyone watching is listed under the video, with a crown on whoever's in control. Click a name to hand it over.
