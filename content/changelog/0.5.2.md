- Uploads no longer stop to say "Hit a snag, retrying…" on almost every clip. The site now waits the second it takes for storage to hand the file over, instead of trying straight away and failing.
- Old WebM clips (like League's built-in recordings) and videos from phones and cameras that used to say "This format needs converting first" now just work. The video is kept exactly as it was; only the sound gets converted so every browser can play it. If any of yours got stuck on that message, upload them again.
- The game box is nicer to use. It keeps your cursor while the site's best guess fills in, tells you when it's searching or found nothing, and shows the game's cover art once you've picked one. The Save button is gone: pick a game and it's saved as that game, never as whatever you'd half typed.
